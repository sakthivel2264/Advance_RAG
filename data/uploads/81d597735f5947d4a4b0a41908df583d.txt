ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

1. Employees are eligible for 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

2. Annual leave is credited at the beginning of each calendar year.

3. Employees must submit annual leave requests to their reporting manager for approval.

4. Employees should submit leave requests at least 5 working days before the planned leave.

5. Unused annual leave may be carried forward, subject to company policy.

6. Annual leave cannot be automatically converted into sick leave.

7. Employees must ensure that their leave balance is sufficient before submitting a request.
