WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Employees may request work-from-home arrangements subject to their team's eligibility rules.

2. Work-from-home requests must be approved by the reporting manager.

3. Employees working remotely must remain available during their assigned working hours.

4. Employees must maintain secure access to company systems while working remotely.

5. Confidential company information must not be accessed through unauthorized devices or networks.

6. Managers may require employees to attend the office for important meetings or business requirements.
