WORKING HOURS POLICY

1. The standard working schedule is 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM from Monday to Friday.

2. Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break.

3. Employees are expected to complete 8 working hours per day, excluding breaks.

4. Any changes to regular working schedules require manager approval.

5. Employees may be required to work additional hours during critical business requirements, subject to applicable company policies.

6. Weekend work requires prior manager approval.
