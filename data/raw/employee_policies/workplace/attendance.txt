ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Employees must record their attendance using the company's attendance management system.

2. Employees are expected to arrive at the workplace on time according to their assigned schedule.

3. Employees must notify their reporting manager if they expect to arrive late.

4. Attendance corrections must be submitted to HR with appropriate justification.

5. Repeated unapproved absences may be escalated to HR.

6. Employees working remotely must follow the attendance reporting requirements.
