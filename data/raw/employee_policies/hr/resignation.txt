RESIGNATION POLICY

1. Employees intending to resign must submit a formal resignation through the company's designated process.

2. The standard notice period is 60 calendar days.

3. Notice period requirements may vary based on employment terms.

4. Early release requests require approval from the reporting manager and HR.

5. Employees must complete knowledge transfer and handover activities before their last working day.

6. Company assets must be returned during the exit clearance process.

7. Final settlement is processed after completion of the required exit formalities.
