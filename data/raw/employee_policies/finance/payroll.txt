PAYROLL POLICY

1. Employee salaries are processed monthly.

2. Salaries are generally credited on the last working day of each month.

3. Salary calculations include applicable earnings, allowances, deductions, and statutory contributions.

4. Employees must review their payslips after salary processing.

5. Payroll discrepancies must be reported to the HR or payroll department.

6. Salary revisions require approval through the company's designated process.
