TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim eligible travel expenses.

2. Business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before the trip.

3. Employees must submit original receipts or valid digital receipts for reimbursement.

4. Travel reimbursement claims should be submitted within 15 calendar days after completing the trip.

5. Personal expenses are not eligible for business travel reimbursement.

6. Reimbursement is subject to the company's expense policy and approval process.
