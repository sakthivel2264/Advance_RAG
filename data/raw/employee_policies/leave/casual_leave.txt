CASUAL LEAVE POLICY

1. Employees are eligible for 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

2. Casual leave is intended for personal matters and unforeseen circumstances.

3. Employees should obtain manager approval before taking planned casual leave.

4. Casual leave should normally not exceed 3 consecutive working days.

5. Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.

6. Casual leave cannot be exchanged for monetary compensation.
