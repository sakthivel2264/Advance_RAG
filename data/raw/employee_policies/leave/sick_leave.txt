SICK LEAVE POLICY

1. Employees may request sick leave when they are unable to work due to illness.

2. Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when taking sick leave.

3. A medical certificate may be requested for extended sick leave.

4. Sick leave requests must be recorded in the company's leave management system.

5. Sick leave approval is subject to the company's HR policy.

6. Employees should provide supporting medical documentation when requested by HR.
